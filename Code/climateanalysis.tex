\#Goals We will examine how climate variables are linked to different
aspects of community state transitions. THis is focused on frog calling
data from 8 different ponds across 22 years. Climate data is taking from
local weather stations, and frog calling data was converted to
transition matrix indicating how community states (i.e.~a specific
combination of species) transition over time within a year.

So two PCA are enough to explain a lot of variation in all these
variables, and the first explains by far the most. It's also interesting
that the correlation between variables are robust and don't change if we
ignore small 1, 2, or 3 day gaps. Based on these relationship, we
definitely want to keep No\_Spec as covariate when we analyze the other
metrics, and there is no point in including both totalEdges and Unique
nodes since both are so correlated, and total Edges seems to be more
different from No species than unique nodes and bit more informative on
other aspects of the network.
